Bringing your progress with you

Everything you studied — the schedule, the answers behind it, and the deck settings — was stored by your browser under this domain, and a browser keeps each domain's storage to itself. It is too much to send along in a link, so it takes a file:

  1. Export it from this page. It downloads a single JSON file.
  2. Open mf.wataash.com, press BACKUP at the top of the deck list, then CHOOSE BACKUP FILE…, pick that file, and press RESTORE.

Do this on every browser and device you studied on: each one has its own progress, and restoring them one after another merges them.
